## Spec changes (dated 2026-10-31)

- schedule operations have their own lifetime family (C-1);
- eight operations, with no Delete in M3 (C-2);
- one 2 MiB ceiling (C-3);
- no scheduler lease (C-4);
- a dedicated `schedule` scope kind (C-5);

### Owed cases closed (2026-11-01)

Product change: `IJobContext.Schedule` (01 §9) is now set. 13 already listed it as required for M3, and until now it was always null.

- The store writes the occurrence into the payload row's `headers` as a `schedule` member, in `PgStore.Dispatch.cs` (`Headers`).
- Reserve reads it back (`PgStore.Reserve.cs`, `PgStore.ReadScheduleHeader`) and the pipeline exposes it (`JobPipeline.cs`).
- `DispatchItem` and `ReservedJob` carry it (`StoreProtocol.cs`). `ScheduleTemplate.ToItem` takes it (`ScheduleProtocol.cs`), and the three schedule dispatch paths pass it (`PgStore.Schedules.cs`).
- A manual trigger passes `default`, and the dispatch core substitutes its decision time. This fixes a defect the hosted test exposed: reading `tx.Now` before the decision gave `0001-01-01`.
- There is no migration and no extra join.

New tests: `SchedulePlannerTests` (Core, Pure, 17 tests) and `ScheduleCaseTests` (PostgreSQL, 11 tests). Runs: the last two Testcontainers runs (`RUNWICK_TEST_PG` unset) of the final test text, after iterating on the dev database. Earlier Testcontainers runs found two test races, both fixed in the tests:

- The SCHED-14 test now slows the clock before rewinding, so the loop cannot materialize first.
- The CMD-04 replay command now has a 6 s rather than a 2 s NotAfter.

The existing `ScheduleStoreTests` all carry `Item=SP-016` with their `Case` traits. They were rerun six times under Testcontainers because Reserve and Dispatch changed. `ScalingTests` passed 6/6 in the same runs. One existing theory row was flaky: `An_episode_of_exactly_the_limit_completes_and_one_more_needs_review(extra: 0)` failed 2 of 6 runs with `ScheduleEvaluationException: The 250 ms evaluation budget elapsed`. Host load average was about 22 on 8 vCPUs, shared with other agents. It is a wall-clock budget hit, not a result of these changes; it passes when the host is not overloaded. Not changed (follow-up: give that test a larger budget or a fake `TimeProvider`).
