# SP-006 acceptance report: dispatch

Date: 2026-10-30. Tag: `sp-006`.

## Delivered

- **Caller-owned staging (spec 01 §15, 04 §6.1).**
  - Public `Runwick.Postgres.IPostgresJobDispatcher` (`StageAsync` ×2, `StageManyAsync` ×2), registered by `UsePostgres`.
  - `Store/PostgresJobDispatcher.cs` runs Dispatch/1 and DispatchMany/1 on the caller's `NpgsqlTransaction`. There is no local admission and no unknown-outcome exception, and the writes never use another connection.
  - The result is `StagedDispatch`/`StagedBatch`, which make no durability claim.
- **Runner refactor (`PgRunner`).**
  - The gate → receipt → body → finalize sequence is factored into `ProtocolAsync`, shared by driver-owned runs and staging.
  - `PgRunner.StageAsync` wraps that sequence in the savepoint `runwick_stage`. A rejection rolls back to the savepoint and leaves the caller's transaction usable.
  - PostgreSQL errors propagate unchanged.
  - A read-only or completed transaction is rejected with `transaction_invalid`; a different store identity with `transaction_store_mismatch`.
- **Lock-wait expiry (CMD-04).** A lock or statement timeout, or a 40P01/40001 rollback, observed at or after `NotAfter` is reported as `CommandExpired` / `ObservedNoEffect` instead of a retryable store error.
- **`PgRunner.TransientConflicts`.** A counter of 40P01/40001/55P03 rollbacks. The runner retries those silently, so the deadlock-freedom tests assert on this counter.
- **`PgStore.Dispatch`.** The bodies and replay codecs are shared by `DispatchAsync`, `DispatchManyAsync`, `StageDispatchAsync` and `StageDispatchManyAsync`.
- **Harness.**
  - `DriverFixture.CountAsync`.
  - Peer `dispatchPrepared`: imports the local process's prepared envelope, so both processes send the same operation.
  - Peer `conflicts` command and the `match` field in dispatch replies.
  - Test contracts `test.capped` (type capacity 1), `test.pair` (two resources), `test.shared` (resource capacity 2), used by SP-007/SP-008.

Spec changes are recorded with dates: 04 P-25 (expiry after a lock wait), 04 P-26 (staging savepoint and error surface), and 07 I-15 (the UNI-04 step order, the CMD-05 simulated-retention bound, and the deadlock evidence counter).

## Notes

- **Staged PostgreSQL errors.** 04 §6.1 says the driver "reports [40001] as not applied". TX-03 requires the error unchanged. The two agree: the caller receives the unchanged `PostgresException`, and nothing staged survives, because the caller's transaction is aborted (P-26).
- **Public `ResolveAsync(operationId)`.** It has no access to the command's NotAfter and bounds by the 10-minute deadline cap, so it reports `NotObserved` inside that bound and `ExpiredUnknown`/`NotApplied` afterwards. Tests that simulate retention passing use the store arbiter with an offset bound (I-15).
