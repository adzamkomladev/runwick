## Fixes found while testing

- **Known release did not lift the queue cooldown (engine; found by RDY-01 in the full-suite run).** A retiring attempt removed its queue from the probe exclusion, but the queue-scope cooldown from the CapacityBlocked reply stayed. Under load, the backlog then drained about two jobs per cooldown or idle period (≤ 2 s): roughly 500 s for 500 jobs, against the 3-minute assertion. Now `OnAttemptRetired` also wakes the pool, and a dequeued release clears that queue's cooldown (`LaneSelector.ReleaseQueue`). Lane cooldowns stay, as 09 §4.2 requires.
- **The draining presence row was never written (engine; found by MODE-P).** The draining publication ran under control stop, and `StopAsync` cancelled control stop before the write finished. `StopAsync` now waits for the publication, bounded by 2 s, before cancelling. A timeout is diagnosed, not thrown.
- RDY-02 (test only): the query read `closed_at`; the column is `finished_at`.
- `PgStore.Readiness`: `IsDBNullAsync` instead of `IsDBNull` (CA1849).
